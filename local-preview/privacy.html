Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how hustlr.chat handles personal information through this website and enquiries you send to us. It covers the website and initial contact; individual services, pilots, or payment providers may have additional privacy notices presented when you use them.

Information we receive

  • Contact enquiries: your name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to include in the contact form.
  • WhatsApp enquiries: if you message us, we receive the phone number, profile information, messages, and attachments that WhatsApp makes available to the recipient.
  • Technical information: hosting and service providers may process your IP address, browser details, requested pages, and request times to deliver and protect the website.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, arrange requested demonstrations, discuss services or partnerships, and follow up on your request. Technical information supports website delivery, troubleshooting, and protection against abuse.
