05 / Why it works

Built for WhatsApp

The way people already find help: ask someone. Hustlr just makes sure the right person answers.

  • No app to install

    If WhatsApp is on your phone, Hustlr is too. Nothing to download, no account to create.

  • Just type it

    No menus or forms. Describe the job the way you would tell a friend.

  • Light on data

    Everything happens in text messages, so a small data bundle goes a long way.

  • See who you are booking

    Each provider comes with their category, the areas they cover and what they do.

  • Nothing happens until you say yes

    Hustlr shows you the booking details first. Reply YES to book, or NO to cancel.

  • Rebook in one reply

    Liked the work? Reply SAVE to keep that provider in your favourites for next time.

06 / Good to know

Questions

The short answers. For anything else, ask in the chat.

Do I need to install anything?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you already have everything you need. Hustlr works entirely inside the chat.

What does it cost?

Asking Hustlr to find someone is free. You agree the price for the job directly with the provider before any work starts.

What happens to my details?

We only share your job, area and contact with the provider you choose, so they can confirm the booking. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Which services can I find?

Plumbers, electricians, cleaners and tutors across Zimbabwe, with more trades on the way. If you are not sure, just ask in the chat.
